# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should be constructive, assume good faith, respect different
experiences, and keep discussion focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberately exposing private
information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to
[support@sealedcash.com](mailto:support@sealedcash.com). Project maintainers
will review reports fairly and may remove comments, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when necessary. Please do not use public issues for
security or conduct reports.
